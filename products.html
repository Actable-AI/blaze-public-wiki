Published product menu on blaze.vn:

  • “Blaze App” — “All-in-one voice AI app” (links to https://app.blaze.vn). Source: https://blaze.vn
  • “Blaze API” — “Voice AI for developers” (links to https://app.blaze.vn/api/documentation/introduction). Source: https://blaze.vn
  • “Blaze Note” — “AI meeting notes” (links to https://note.blaze.vn). Source: https://blaze.vn

Homepage feature tabs: AI Voice Assistant, Text-to-Speech, Speech-to-Text, Live Interpreter, Live Audio News. Source: https://blaze.vn

  • AI Voice Assistant: “Deploy an AI voice assistant that listens, understands context, and responds naturally in real time. Blaze combines Speech-to-Text, LLM reasoning, and low-latency Text-to-Speech to support customer care, internal helpdesk, and voice automation workflows.” Source: https://blaze.vn
  • Text-to-Speech: “Convert written text into natural, human-like speech.” “Our AI Text-to-Speech model supports regional accents from across the country, voice cloning with just a 5-second sample, and an API for real-time voicebots. The model can be deployed for businesses either on the cloud or on-premise.” Source: https://blaze.vn
  • Speech-to-Text: “Accurate speech transcription service.” “High-accuracy South East Asian speech recognition with real-time processing.” Source: https://blaze.vn
  • Live Interpreter: “Break language barriers with instant AI-powered interpretation.” Demo pair shown: EN ↔ VI. Source: https://blaze.vn
  • Live Audio News: “Deliver news in audio format, updated in real time. Listen to the news anytime, anywhere—while driving or cooking—no screen needed.” Source: https://blaze.vn
  • Blaze Note on the main site: “AI meeting notes, written for you.” “Blaze Note records and transcribes your meetings, then turns them into clear summaries, key points, and action items — powered by Blaze Speech-to-Text for Vietnamese and Southeast Asian languages.” Sub-features: “Record & transcribe” (“accurate, speaker-aware transcription in Vietnamese and English”); “AI summaries & action items”; “Search & share”. Source: https://blaze.vn
