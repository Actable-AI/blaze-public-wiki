Blaze App pricing

Page title: “Simple pricing based on your needs”. “Choose the plan that fits your usage. Easy upgrades, dedicated support.” Source: https://blaze.vn/pricing

Starter — “Free trial, suitable for basic experience.” “$0 Monthly”.

  • Receive 1,000 credits
  • Generate voices with 30 diverse voice styles
  • Convert voice to text
  • Support 2 languages: English & Vietnamese
  • Use up to 1 processing stream at a time

Source: https://blaze.vn/pricing

Team — “For small businesses with growing needs.” “$10 Monthly”. “Includes Starter plan, plus” “+18,000 credits” and “Support 2 languages: English & Vietnamese”. The same card also lists these credit figures without a stated dollar amount for each: 2,000 Credits, 7,500 Credits, 19,000 Credits, 43,000 Credits, 168,000 Credits, 558,000 Credits, 14,558,000 Credits. Source: https://blaze.vn/pricing

Enterprise — “Maximum plan for large businesses with priority support.” Price: “Custom”.

  • Custom payment terms
  • Security review support
  • Flexible limits and controls
  • Support 2 languages: English & Vietnamese
  • Use up to 1 processing stream at a time

Source: https://blaze.vn/pricing

FAQ on the same page (JSON-LD; first answer also visible in the accordion):

  • “Do credits have an expiration date?” — “Blaze App: Credits in the Free and Blaze Pro plans are refreshed monthly. Additional purchased credits are only valid during the current Blaze Pro subscription cycle. Blaze API Platform: Credits do not expire.”
  • “Are Blaze App and Blaze API Platform two separate systems?” — “Yes, the Blaze App and the Blaze API Platform are two independent systems. You cannot use credits between these two systems.”
  • “Can the number of credits be adjusted when purchasing?” — “Yes, the number of credits may vary depending on the time of purchase, for example, during promotional programs or due to policies adjusting the number of credits at that time.”
  • “Can I use credits for all services (TTS, STT, etc.) on the Blaze App?” — “Yes, on the Blaze App, credits can be used flexibly for all services such as TTS, STT, etc.”
  • “Can enterprises customize their service plans?” — “Yes, the Enterprise Custom package allows businesses to customize the service to their needs. Please contact us for detailed advice.”

Source: https://blaze.vn/pricing

Note: the FAQ names “Free and Blaze Pro”; the visible plan cards are titled Starter, Team, and Enterprise. No Blaze Pro price is published on the card list.
