Legal — privacy

  • Applies when using Blaze AI services. Source: https://blaze.vn/privacy-policy
  • Account Data: Blaze AI is Data Controller. Includes name, email, phone if any, payment info, account user info, transaction history, settings, contact info. Basis: contract; legitimate interest. Source: https://blaze.vn/privacy-policy
  • Customer Data via Blaze API: Blaze AI is Data Processor; customer is Data Controller. Includes input, output, reference voice, embeddings, recordings, transcripts, request metadata sent via https://api.blaze.vn (TTS, STT, Diarization, Voice Profiler and related APIs). DPA prevails over this policy for Customer Data if they conflict. Source: https://blaze.vn/privacy-policy
  • “Blaze API KHÔNG bao gồm Blaze Web App, Blaze Note, Blaze Dubbing hoặc các sản phẩm khác của Blaze. Các sản phẩm đó có Điều khoản Sử dụng và Chính sách Bảo mật riêng.” Source: https://blaze.vn/privacy-policy
  • Customer must have a legal basis and valid consent before sending personal data, including sensitive data (biometric voice, voice samples, speaker_id) and data of children under 16 (verifiable parent/guardian consent). Source: https://blaze.vn/privacy-policy
  • Zero Data Retention for API Customer Data: content is processed in RAM or ephemeral cache and deleted immediately after the result is returned. Blaze says it does not store audio/text/output to disk or database, does not back up customer content, does not log audio/transcript/text content, and does not store speaker_id, voice profile, or voice embeddings after the request completes. Technical logs (timestamp, request ID, error code, request size, model name, IP, API key hash) kept up to 30 days and “KHÔNG chứa bất kỳ nội dung audio, văn bản, transcript, giọng nói, hoặc dữ liệu đầu ra nào.” Source: https://blaze.vn/privacy-policy
  • “Toàn bộ Dữ liệu Khách hàng qua Blaze API được xử lý hoàn toàn tại Việt Nam. Blaze AI KHÔNG chuyển giao dữ liệu cá nhân ra ngoài lãnh thổ Việt Nam.” Source: https://blaze.vn/privacy-policy
  • Cookies: site uses cookies; users can control them in the browser. Source: https://blaze.vn/privacy-policy
  • Contact for data-subject rights: support@blaze.vn; business address Tầng 10, Tòa nhà VET, số 98 Hoàng Quốc Việt, Hà Nội. Source: https://blaze.vn/privacy-policy

No separate Blaze Note privacy policy page was found on note.blaze.vn. Note marketing describes encryption and org isolation but does not repeat the API ZDR text.
