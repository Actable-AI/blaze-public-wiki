Blaze public knowledge base

For answering customer questions. Every fact links back to a public page. Nothing here is private account data.

Pricing conflict on Blaze Note. The plan cards on note.blaze.vn/pricing say the free plan is 6 hours per session. The FAQ on the same page says 60 minutes per session, and also cites 25.000đ/session (3 hours) and 399.000đ/month unlimited. Do not pick one. Quote both, or send the customer to that page.
Blaze Note pricing page update (6 Oct 2026). note.blaze.vn/pricing now also lists Gói Nhóm at 99.000đ / chỗ / tháng (từ 2 chỗ), two enterprise cards (Private Cloud and On-premise, “Liên hệ”), and credit rates of 1 / 2 / 3 credit per minute. Personal cards are still 199.000đ and 399.000đ per month. See Blaze Note pricing.
Meeting API billing (docs update). Public developer docs on note.blaze.vn now bill the Note Meeting / AI API in prepaid VND (hours and tokens), not app “credits.” See Blaze Note Meeting API.
